Arrivals that fade out instead of vanishing, and a notification for the bus you are waiting for.

## What's new

- **Arrivals that fade out**: a bus that reaches the stop no longer vanishes from the list. It reads **At the stop**, then **Passed 1 min ago**, greyed below the buses still coming, and goes a few minutes later. This works on the home, the stops screen and a stop's details. In the trip planner, a bus still at the stop counts if you can reach it.
- **The bus you are waiting for**: tap the bell on an arrival to follow that bus in a notification until it comes. It shows the minutes left and alerts you at about 2 minutes, then shows **At the stop** and asks **Did you take it?**
  - **I took it**, **Stop waiting**, swiping it away or tapping the bell again ends it.
  - Up to five buses at once, each with its own notification.
  - On Android 16 and later it is a Live Update, with the minutes in the status bar.
  - It keeps going with the app closed and the screen off, and leaves nothing behind once it ends.
- **Alerts**: silent by default. Settings > Alerts adds a sound and a vibration to the alert when the bus is near; Android's own notification settings still have the last word.
- **One more fare owing**: when the balance left after the whole fares still lets you board once more and owe it, a card shows **(+1)** after its fares, as in "4 FARES (+1)".
- **Fix**: relinking a stop while its buses were loading could be undone by that load; now it holds, and the new stop's buses show at once.

## Installation

Download either APK (they are the same file) and install it over 5.0.0: your cards, stops and settings stay. From the app: Settings > About > **Check for updates**. The first tap on a bell asks to allow notifications.
