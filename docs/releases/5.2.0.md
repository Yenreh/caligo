Saved places for one-tap trips, one notification per stop for the buses you wait for, and a quicker, sturdier planner and map.

## What's new

- **Saved places**: in the trip planner, the star beside a place saves it under a short name, such as Home or Work.
  - Its chip then plans a trip there from where you are with one tap.
  - The place picker lists your places first.
  - A long press renames or deletes one, with Undo.
  - Backups carry them with your cards and stops.
- **One notification per stop**: waiting for several buses at the same stop shows them together, soonest first, as in "E21 5 min · T31 7 min".
  - It has **E21: I took it**, **T31: I took it** and **Stop waiting** for all of them, and taking one ends the others there.
  - As a bus passes, it asks **Did you take the E21?**
  - Buses at other stops keep their own notification.
- **On the lock screen**: the buses you wait for now show there too, still silent.
- **A quicker planner**: it starts from where the phone was last seen when that is recent, and refines it quietly, with no spinner. The location button still asks for a precise fix.
- **Maps that keep working**: if CARTO stops serving the map's tiles, every map switches to OpenStreetMap, and tries CARTO again 10 minutes later.
- **Fewer requests**: waits at the platforms of one station share one request, and after a failure the app waits longer before asking again instead of retrying every round.
- **Fixes**:
  - An import with a stop off the map, or with overlong names, is refused instead of breaking later exports.
  - The minutes in a wait's notification stay on one line with their number.
  - At large text, the planner's title shrinks a little instead of being cut.

## Installation

Download either APK (they are the same file) and install it over 5.1.0: your cards, stops and settings stay. From the app: Settings > About > **Check for updates**.
